r1_hdr: Home Run Derby: Rules & Controls
r1_h_rule: Rules
r1_rule: Take 10 swings. Your score is the total distance of your 5 longest hits. Fouls and misses count as 0 m.
r1_h_ctrl: Controls
r1_c1: Use the D-pad to set Shoma's position in the box.
r1_c2: {icons} Swing the bat.
r1_k1: Move Shoma
r1_k2: Swing
r1_back: Back
r2a_hdr: PK Shootout: Rules & Controls
r2a_h_rule: Rules
r2a_rule: Play a PK shootout as Roberto. Each side takes 5 kicks, and whoever scores more wins.
r2a_h_ctrl: Kicker Controls
r2a_c1: Use the D-pad to aim your kick.
r2a_c2: {icons} Any of these starts Roberto's run-up.
r2a_c3: Until you kick, use the D-pad to set how much the ball curves.
r2a_k1: Explosive V Goal|(uses the Guts gauge)
r2a_k2: Strong Shot
r2a_k3: Weak Shot
r2a_k4: Explosive V Goal|(uses the Guts gauge)
r2a_k5: Aim gauge ON/OFF
r2a_k6: Change the view.
r2a_back: Back
r2b_hdr: PK Shootout: Rules & Controls
r2b_h_ctrl: Keeper Controls
r2b_c1: Use the D-pad to move.
r2b_c2: D-pad + a button makes a save attempt. Each button does a different move.
r2b_k1: Shining Save (Strong)|(uses the Guts gauge)
r2b_k2: Strong Save
r2b_k3: Weak Save
r2b_k4: Shining Save (Weak)|(uses the Guts gauge)
r2b_gauge: Aim Gauge
r2b_gauge_t: Left/right kick angle|Up/down kick angle
r2b_back: Back
r3_hdr: Explosive Shot: Rules & Controls
r3_h_rule: Rules
r3_rule: Shoot to knock down the score panels in the goal. The total of 6 balls counts. Besides the panel points, hitting a post to knock a panel down earns Frame points, and curving the ball into a panel earns Art points.
r3_h_ctrl: Controls
r3_c1: Use the D-pad to aim your shot.
r3_c2: {icons} Either one starts Roberto's run-up.
r3_c3: Until you shoot, use the D-pad to set how much the ball curves.
r3_k1: Strong Shot
r3_k2: Weak Shot
r3_k3: Change the view.
r3_back: Back
r4_hdr: Service Ace: Rules & Controls
r4_h_rule: Rules
r4_rule: Serve at the face characters and catch them in the blast to score. The total of 8 serves counts.
r4_h_ctrl: Controls
r4_c1: Use Left/Right on the D-pad to aim the serve.
r4_c2: {icons} Press to set the power.
r4_c3: How long you hold the button sets the serve angle.
r4_c4: Release the button to serve.
r4_power: Power Gauge
r4_power_t: Sets the serve power.
r4_angle: Angle Gauge
r4_angle_t: Sets the serve's up/down angle.
r4_back: Back
